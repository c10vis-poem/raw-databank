## What was decided, so it isn't relitigated

- **Compilation ≠ compression.** Compilation is word-for-word translation, one
  file in, one file out. Compression is a later step that merges duplicate and
  superseded versions. A summary is neither.
- **The operator's typed turns are the primary content** in every chat export.
  Model turns are drafts, most of them rejected. Preserve both, mark the
  boundary, judge neither.
- **The check is the operator's** — a different tool reads the original and the
  cleaned version and names what's missing. RLVR: the score is real because
  "is this detail present in both" is mechanically verifiable.
- **CLAUDE.md is the router**, not the wiki. The **handoff** carries session
  state. The wiki is running knowledge, built later.
- **Horizons-Ui is the most independent** of the three components, not a shell
  that depends on the daemons. Documents claiming otherwise are superseded.
- **Nothing comes from `NovA-Corpus`.** 81% of it predates 2026-08-05.
- **Never stall on a tool.** If it fails, do the job another way.
